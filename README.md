# ASTEROID — Neon Dodge

A little neon arcade game. Fly, dodge, shoot.

### ▶ [Play it here](https://remagent001.github.io/asteroid/)

## Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| **Arrow keys** or **WASD** | Fly |
| **Space** | Shoot (hold to auto-fire) |
| **Mouse / finger drag** | Also flies the ship — works on phones |

## How it works

You get 3 lives. Your score climbs while you stay alive, **+50** for every asteroid
you shoot, and **+25** for each one that falls past you. Big asteroids break into two
smaller chunks instead of vanishing, so clearing a large one takes three shots — you
either spend the ammo or fly around it.

It gets faster and busier for about the first 55 seconds, then holds steady, so there's
a ceiling worth chasing rather than a wall you always hit. Your best score is saved in
your browser.

There's a light/dark toggle at the top of the play area.

## The whole thing is one file

`index.html` is the entire game — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and even the favicon, with no
libraries and no build step. Download it and double-click it; it runs offline, forever.
